## Profile

Experimental physicist (PhD, JGU Mainz) who builds and fixes the equipment other people depend on. For about five years I was the local owner of a €0.5–1.0M thin-film deposition and measurement facility — I integrated new instruments into it, kept it running, and trained 10–12 researchers to operate it independently. I work comfortably across thin-film process development, measurement-system building, and data analysis, and I am now moving that hands-on toolkit from research into industrial R&D and production.

## Core competencies

**Thin-film process & deposition** — DC/RF and pulsed magnetron sputtering, pulsed laser deposition (PLD), thermal evaporation, reactive sputtering; process optimization for deposition rate, film uniformity and etch profile; 200 mm wafer deposition on a production-capable UHV platform (SINGULUS ROTARIS, operated in R&D/pilot mode).

**Characterization & metrology** — XRD/XRR, AFM, SEM, SQUID/VSM, MOKE, FMR; low-temperature (down to ~2 K), high-field magnetotransport; wafer-level thickness (XRR) and magnetic-uniformity mapping across 20–30 mm and 200 mm wafers.

**Instrumentation & vacuum systems** — high- and ultra-high-vacuum systems; RHEED integration and alignment; cryostats and superconducting magnets; RF/microwave measurement to ~20 GHz; four-probe stations, wire bonding; design-for-build CAD for vacuum hardware.

**Automation & data** — Python (pandas, NumPy, matplotlib) for multi-TB experimental datasets, batch processing, nonlinear least-squares fitting (scipy.optimize) and basic machine-learning signal classification; extended the group's LabVIEW instrument-control software; HDF5 data structures, eLabFTW electronic lab notebooks; OriginPro, LaTeX, KLayout.

**Fabrication & cleanroom** — optical and e-beam lithography, ion-beam etching (IBE/RIBE), lift-off, annealing; device integration, electrical contacting and probe-station screening.

## Selected projects

Three builds where I re-architected the lab rather than just using it. Full portfolio at [a-akashdeep.github.io/work](/work/).

**RHEED-into-PLD integration.** Integrated a reflection high-energy electron diffraction system into a pulsed-laser-deposition chamber end to end: spatial-constraint analysis, multiple CAD layout iterations reviewed with the group head, chamber relocation, a custom support structure and dedicated pumping unit, electron-beam alignment, and commissioning into routine use with operating procedures.

**Cryostat sample-stage sensor.** The cryostat's thermometry was reading a different temperature than the sample itself. I mounted a calibrated sensor directly at the sample position — wiring, electronics and calibration — which removed a systematic temperature artifact and made the electrical and magnetic data trustworthy.

**Vacuum transfer suitcase.** Specified and procured the active and passive pumps and a transfer arm, CAD-designed the parts for the workshop, then assembled, tested and calibrated a vacuum-preserving sample-transfer path compatible with both the PLD and sputtering chambers.
